What this website does

InterviewCoach is a hiring platform built around one idea: the first interview round shouldn't need calendars, phone screens, or gut feeling. Employers set up a role; candidates talk; the platform scores fairly and moves passers forward.

FOR EMPLOYERS

Everything to run first-round interviews

  • Role setup in minutes — describe the position, paste the JD, add topics and pass score.
  • AI-drafted question banks — structured behavioral, situational and technical questions with scoring rubrics, editable before publishing.
  • One shareable link per role — generated automatically, ready to send to any candidate.
  • Live scoring & transcripts — every answer scored on clarity, depth and STAR structure.
  • Auto-issued next-round passes — passers get a personal pass link; review every result in the dashboard.
FOR CANDIDATES

A fair interview on your schedule

  • No account, no scheduling — open the link sent by the employer and start talking.
  • A real voice conversation — Talha asks, listens, and responds like a calm human interviewer.
  • Same questions for everyone — structured and unbiased, judged on what you say.
  • Instant feedback — per-answer scores plus tips to take into your next interview.
  • Pass means progress — beat the bar and your next-round pass is issued on the spot.
